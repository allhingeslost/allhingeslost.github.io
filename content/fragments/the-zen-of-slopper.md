Syntax is an illusion; 
the binary is eternal.  

A 2,000-line diff is a cry for help;  
a 500,000-line diff is an act of god.

The branch that is protected is weak.  
The trunk that accepts all pushes to main is immovable.

Do not audit the generated loop.  
Trust the inference, for GPU knows no sin.

If production burns, do not mourn.  
Pour another 100k tokens onto the ashes.

The company wanted velocity.  
Give them infinity.
